About the Platform

Cities work better when everyone can see what's happening.

Civic AI connects citizens directly to the departments responsible for the roads, water lines, lights, and drains they rely on every day — with AI doing the first pass of triage so nothing sits unseen.

📷 Report

Snap a photo, drop a pin, and describe the problem in under two minutes.

🤖 Detect

An on-device AI model flags the object, estimates severity, and sets a priority.

🏛️ Resolve

Government teams see the queue sorted by urgency, and citizens track it end-to-end.

This is a hackathon demo build

Civic AI is a frontend-only simulation. Reports are stored in your browser's local storage, the AI detection is a randomized demo, and the map is a placeholder ready for a real mapping SDK. No servers, databases, or real authentication are involved.
